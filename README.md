A. Case 1 – Amoxicillin

Patient: 25-year-old male

Condition: Bacterial throat infection

Drug: Amoxicillin 500 mg

Symptoms: Skin rash and itching

Timeline: Symptoms developed 3 days after starting treatment.

Suspected ADR: Possible allergic skin reaction

Suspected drug: Amoxicillin

Reasoning:
The symptoms appeared after initiation of the medicine and
improved after discontinuation. Therefore, amoxicillin is
suspected as the cause.
